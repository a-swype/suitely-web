Where the registrant picks up

Everything above was done before the file was assigned. From here it is entirely yours:

  • Attend the four viewings on Saturday and host them.
  • Carry the trade and the client relationship.
  • Review, amend and sign off the Agreement to Lease we draft — nothing goes to a client or a co-operating brokerage without you.

Supervision. The trade sits with your brokerage and your broker supervises it. We produce drafts and recommendations, not decisions.

What the coordination actually cost 9 days, before a registrant was needed

  • 2 OctIntake — brief captured, information guide delivered and acknowledged, representation agreement signed. Search live within the hour.
